Experience

Bishop's Store House (volunteer work)

At the Bishop’s Store House I gained a lot of experience in customer service and maintaining a store environment. For example, I assisted and guided customers to help collect all the items and food assigned to them on their list. While these people were not exactly customers, they had to collect and check out their food in the same way they would in a grocery store. I had to learn to prevent as many mistakes as possible and be professional when communicating. Along with that, I assisted other workers with cleaning up at closing time and assisted other workers with setup before opening time. I learned how to make sure the store looks good and keep things clean. Also, I collected items from the back of the store and restocked shelves. And lastly, I weighed and bagged produce, vegetables, and fruits according to instructions. This gave me valuable experience in practicing food safety.

NJROTC

For two years at my high school, I was part of the NJROTC program. Thanks to two great teachers, I learned a lot and participated in valuable activities during the class and outside of school. For one, we had many lessons on practicing leadership, and it was a requirement to do 40 hours of volunteer work a year. Some of that volunteer work I spent being a night guard for football events at my school, where I watched and patrolled for people breaking rules, assisted people who needed help, and controlled traffic.

Education

School has given me a lot of opportunity to develop a wide range of skills. In my AP Statistics class and my college Math in Society class, I learned skills for using math to solve common problems in the real world. In an Intro to Culinary class, I learned knife safety and food safety regulations, and practiced safety in the kitchen. For creative skills, I took two basic art classes, a graphics design class, and two classes for creative writing. I learned the basic skills for an artist such as color theory, how to use many different types of drawing/painting materials, how to use adobe photoshop and illustrator, properties of good design, how to appeal and work with customers, and more. In my creative writing classes, I got a lot of practice on grammar and typing, as well as knowledge on the many types of writing forms, and how to make a good story.
